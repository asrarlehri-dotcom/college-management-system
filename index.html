🗄 Data Management

Dummy/demo data test karne ke liye generate karein, phir sirf usay clear kar dein — aapka asal (real) data untouched rahega. Real data ka backup bhi le saktay hain aur baad mein restore kar saktay hain.

🧪 Dummy / Demo Data

10 Departments, 40 Courses, 50 Faculty, 40 Students (real-looking names), full results & fee records.

💾 Backup & Restore (real data)

Download a full backup file, or restore everything from a previously saved backup.

⚠️ Danger Zone

Permanently deletes EVERYTHING (dummy AND real data) — including the cloud database. Cannot be undone unless you have a backup.

🔐 Access Control (Guest Mode)

Guest woh visitor hai jo login nahi hai. By default guest sirf VIEW kar sakta hai (add/edit/delete hamesha login mangega). Yahan se decide karein guest kin pages ko dekh sakta hai. Users & Settings page hamesha sirf Super Admin ke liye reserved hain.

🖼 Dashboard Background Slider

Dashboard ke bilkul upar full-width image slider dikhta hai. Ek ya zyada images upload karein — 1 se zyada ho to khud-ba-khud har few seconds mein slide badalti hai.
